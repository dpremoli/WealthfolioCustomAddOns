# Payslips & Pension add-on

Reads your **SAP payslip PDFs entirely inside Wealthfolio**, keeps the figures per UK tax year,
matches each payslip's net pay to the salary deposit already in your bank account, shows income
tax, National Insurance and student loan per tax year, tracks USS pension contributions and
statements, and (only if you opt in, after a preview) records payroll in its own Wealthfolio account.

Part of the [Wealthfolio custom add-ons monorepo](../../README.md). Requires **Wealthfolio 3.11+**
(add-on SDK 3.11.0).

## Privacy

- **Parsing happens in the add-on sandbox.** The PDF is read in the page (`File` bytes) and parsed
  by a small hand-written text extractor in this add-on. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.
- **No `network` and no `secrets` permission.** The manifest asks for neither, so the host would
  refuse any attempt.
- **What is kept** (in the add-on's own storage): pay date, tax period, tax code, the payment and
  deduction lines (description and amount), the totals, net pay, the "This Period" and "To date"
  figures, and any validation warnings.
- **What is never kept, logged or shown:** employee name, NI number, employee number, tax
  reference, tax office. The PDF itself is not stored.
- **Fixtures are synthetic.** The test payslips are generated by `src/test-utils.ts` from made-up
  names and numbers; the repository is public and contains no real payslip.

## How to use

1. **Import.** Open **Payslips** in the sidebar, then drop PDF files on the import card or press
   *Choose PDF files* (several at once is fine). Each file shows what was imported or why it was
   not; the other files still import. A payslip with the same pay date replaces the stored one.
   Checks (payments and deductions add up, tax and NI agree with the "This Period" figures) never
   block storing; a mismatch shows as a warning on the row.
2. **Salary matching.** For each payslip the add-on looks for a GBP deposit equal to the net pay,
   within a few days of the pay date (default 4, set in Settings), in the accounts you chose
   (default: your active GBP cash accounts). One close match is shown as *Matched*; several
   equally close rows are *Ambiguous*, and you pick the right one; none is *Missing*. Automatic
   matches are recomputed each time; only your manual picks and tags are stored.
   *Tag deposit* (matched rows) adds `[ref:payslip:<pay date>]` to the bank row's comment so you
   can find it again. Nothing else about the row changes.
3. **Tax.** Pick a tax year: gross before USS, USS sacrifice, taxable pay, income tax, employee NI,
   student loan and net pay, effective rates, a month-by-month chart, the year-to-date figures from
   the latest payslip and the months that have no payslip.
4. **Pension.** USS contributions per tax year and in total, from your payslips. Add the figures
   from your USS statements (date, DB annual pension, DB lump sum, optional DC pot and employer
   contribution). The page shows an *illustrative* DB capital value (annual pension x 20 + lump
   sum); it is an estimate, is never written to Wealthfolio and is not part of your net worth. For
   projections use Wealthfolio's own retirement planner (*Add retirement income* / *Add pension
   fund*); this add-on does not rebuild it.
5. **Payroll account (optional, off by default).** In **Settings**, tick *Enable the Payroll account
   tools*, press *Preview* to see every row that would be written and which bank deposits would be
   converted, then *Record in Wealthfolio*. Nothing is written before that click. Each payslip
   without warnings becomes, in a cash account named **Payroll**: gross pay in (a deposit), income
   tax and employee NI (tax rows), each other deduction (withdrawal), and the net pay out as a
   transfer. The transfer is linked to the matched bank deposit, which becomes a transfer in.
   Recording twice adds nothing; rows Wealthfolio refuses are kept and retried on the next record,
   and the reason is shown.

   **Spending caveat.** Keep the Payroll account switched off for Spending in Wealthfolio's
   settings. Linking turns the bank's salary deposit into a transfer, so Wealthfolio's spending
   report may no longer count it as income. (No add-on API sets that switch, and the host's exact
   behaviour here has not been verified.)

   **To undo:** delete the Payroll account's activities (or the account) in Wealthfolio, then open
   the bank account's former salary row and change it back from a transfer in to a deposit (unlink
   it first if Wealthfolio shows it as linked).

## Limits

- Only the **SAP payslip layout** this add-on was written for is understood; anything else is
  reported as "Not a recognised SAP payslip layout".
- **Text PDFs only.** Scanned or image-only PDFs, and encrypted PDFs, are rejected with a message.
- **Not yet tested on real payslips inside Wealthfolio.** It is tested against synthetic PDFs
  and a fake host that mimics 3.11's import and transfer-link behaviour. Check the first import
  against your own payslip before relying on it.
- The **DB capital value is illustrative** (pension x 20 + lump sum).
- **No DC snapshot mirroring yet:** DC pot values are recorded in statements and displayed only;
  they are not mirrored into a Holdings account.

## Permissions

| Permission | Functions | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `accounts` | `getAll`, `create` | List accounts to find the one that received each salary; create the optional Payroll account |
| `activities` | `getAll`, `import`, `saveMany`, `getTransferPair`, `linkTransfer` | Find the deposit matching each payslip, tag it on request, and (opt-in) record payroll and link the net-pay transfer |
| `ui` | `router.add`, `navigation.navigate` | Show the add-on's pages |

No `network`, `secrets`, `spending` or `snapshots` permission.

pnpm test
